About me

Curious by nature.
User-driven by choice.

"I don't just ask what to build — I ask why a person would need it, and what would make it feel effortless."

I'm a frontend engineer who gets genuinely excited about problems. Not just technical ones — the ones that sit at the intersection of a user's frustration and an engineer's blank canvas. Over nine years, that curiosity has taken me through banking dashboards, payment flows, open banking SDKs, browser extensions, and AI-powered tools. Each project taught me something the last one didn't.

Banking is a domain I've grown to love precisely because the stakes are real. When someone checks their balance, transfers money, or onboards to a new financial product, they're trusting you completely. That responsibility pushes me to think from the end user's perspective first — before the component tree, before the API contract, before the sprint ticket. What does this person actually need? Where will they hesitate? What would make this feel safe and simple?

I stay close to where products are headed, not just where they are. I've been exploring AI integrations, building SDK packages that developers love to use, and staying sharp through certifications in GitHub Copilot and modern JavaScript. I believe the best engineers are always students — curious about what changed, what broke somewhere else, and what a smarter solution might look like.
